# **App Name**: RoastArena

## Core Features:

- Arena Marketplace: A live feed of 'Boss' funded roast challenges displayed as premium battle cards with real-time countdowns and USDC prize pools.
- AI Judge Score Tool: A sophisticated tool that uses AI to analyze roasts for humor, savagery, and creativity, providing detailed reasoning for each score generated.
- Live Arena Leaderboard: An esports-inspired dynamic ranking system that updates in real-time as new submissions are scored by the AI Judge.
- Animated Submission Interface: A game-like arena submission experience featuring live character counting and a dramatic AI evaluation sequence.
- Founder Boss Dashboard: Allows 'Boss' users to create new arena challenges, fund prize pools with USDC, and track creator engagement analytics.
- Creator Hall of Fame: A prestige-driven section showcasing historic top-performing roasts and all-time leading earners in the arena.
- Web3 Payout System: A visual reward flow system showing active USDC pools and facilitating reward distributions to top-ranked leaderboard creators.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color is a high-vibration Neon Purple (#BC13FE) to capture the electric energy of a digital arena. Background is a deeply desaturated near-black (#0B0A0C) to support the premium dark-mode aesthetic. Accent color is an Electric Blue (#13C1FE) to distinguish technical elements and highlights.
- We recommend a font pairing: 'Space Grotesk' for futuristic, tech-inspired headlines and 'Inter' for highly readable data-driven leaderboard and dashboard text.
- Utilize aggressive, game-like vector iconography with neon glow borders to signify ranks (Gold, Silver, Bronze) and 'Boss' levels.
- High-contrast esports client layout using glassmorphism cards and dramatic spacing to focus on active competition and leaderboard data.
- Use battle-inspired micro-interactions, such as glowing pulses for leaders and 'sizzling' red/orange visual effects for high-savagery AI scores.
